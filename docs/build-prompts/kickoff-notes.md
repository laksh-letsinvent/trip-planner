## Chief Researcher's Survey

Questions:
1. What is the BEST place you have ever been on holiday?
2. What is the most boring thing grown-ups make you do on holiday?
3. Pick your perfect day: beach / pool / theme park / big city / mountains?
4. How many ice cream stops should one day have? 1 / 2 / 3 or more?
