# Theme Glow-Up — options for the Chief Designer

> For Laranya. Papa thinks the app could look a bit more POW 🎆 — but the theme is yours, so nothing changes unless you pick it. All three options use YOUR Candy Pop colours from DESIGN.md, just turned up louder. Pick one, mix them, or say "it's fine as it is" — all are allowed. Papa can build any of these in under an hour.

## The problem (as Papa sees it)

Right now the background is plain white everywhere, so your Candy Pop colours only show up in small places (buttons and chips). The app can feel more like a form than like Pip's world.

## Option A — Candy Sky 🍬☁️

The background stops being white and becomes a super-soft candy gradient (pale pink melting into pale yellow, like sunrise on sweets). Cards stay white so they float on top like clouds. Smallest change, instantly warmer.

## Option B — Sticker Party ⭐

Everything becomes a sticker, like Pip himself: cards and buttons get a chunky navy outline (like your drawing linework), chips get a tiny tilt like someone stuck them on slightly wonky, and little stars sparkle in the corners of the page. Matches Pip's art style the most.

## Option C — Big Candy Header 🌊

A big teal wavy band across the top of every screen, with Pip peeking over the edge of the wave. The rest of the page stays white and calm below it. Makes the top of the app feel like a splash.

## One more decision only you can make

Your "PIP THE PENGUIN" badge is now the app's browser icon (favicon) and sits at the top of the results page. Where else should it live? Top corner of every screen? Only on the About page? You're the boss of this.

## How to answer

Tell Papa: "A", "B", "C", a mix ("A + the stars from B"), or "leave it". He'll build it, then you check it against DESIGN.md like always. Whatever you pick, update DESIGN.md so it stays the single source of truth.
